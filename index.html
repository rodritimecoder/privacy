About rclone

rclone is designed to link google drive to ssd. It enables users to view, analyze, organize, export data through an intuitive streamlined interface.

Key Features

  • Sync: Syncs files between desktop and google drive.
  • Local: Local Caching.
  • Remote: Remote access.

Google Integration & Data Access Transparency

To provide these features, rclone requests user authorization to connect with your Google account via OAuth 2.0. We request access strictly to deliver user-facing functionality:

  • Google Calendar (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events): Requested strictly to read existing appointments and schedule new task deadlines directly to your primary calendar.
  • Basic Profile (openid, email, profile): Requested strictly to identify your account and personalize your user dashboard.
Our Commitment to Data Safety:

rclone only accesses data with your explicit permission. We do not sell your personal data to third parties, use your data for advertising, or use your data to train non-personalized generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
